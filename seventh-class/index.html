Mother Teresa

Biography

Mother Teresa (born Agnes Gonxha Bojaxhiu on August 26, 1910, in Skopje, now in North Macedonia) was a Roman Catholic nun and missionary known around the world for her lifelong work helping the poorest of the poor. She founded the Missionaries of Charity in Calcutta (Kolkata), India, in 1950 an organization dedicated to serving the sick, dying, homeless, and destitute through hospices, shelters, schools, and clinics. For her humanitarian service, she received many honors, including the 1979 Nobel Peace Prize. She became a saint of the Catholic Church in 2016 and is remembered as a powerful symbol of compassion and charity.
